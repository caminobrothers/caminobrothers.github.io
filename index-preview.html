Buen camino, and welcome. We are Nicki, Mike and Dennis, and since 2019 we've walked eight caminos across Spain, Portugal, Germany and Austria together — a few thousand kilometres of trail, countless café con leches, and a brotherhood forged on the way. This site documents each one: the routes, the dates, the stories, and the photos.

The Caminos

  • Portuguese (2019)Sep 29 – Oct 12, 2019 Porto → Santiago · 240 km — where it all began.
  • Francés (2021)Oct 3 – Oct 21, 2021 León → Finisterre · 413 km — the reunion after Covid; Mike and Nicki walked.
  • Rheinsteig (2022)Aug 5 – Aug 19, 2022 Biebrich Castle → Sayn · 195 km along the Rhine — Dennis is back, all three together.
  • Portuguese (2023)May 5 – May 19, 2023 Porto → Finisterre via the Spiritual Variant · 340 km — and a surprise visit from Mike's daughter.
  • Madeira (2024)May 8 – May 17, 2024 One base in Funchal, a new levada walk every day — the island week.
  • Jakobsweg (2024)Oct 11 – Oct 21, 2024 Munich → Innsbruck · 155 km — three pilgrim trails stitched into one.
  • Norte / Francés (2025)Jul 10 – Jul 24, 2025 Irun → Santiago — two trails, one summer; Mike and Nicki.
  • Tyrol (2026)Jun 5 – Jun 19, 2026 Salzburg → Munich through Tyrol and the Allgäu — the hardest ever.
  • Coming SoonSep 28 – Oct 18, 2027 Camino 9: the Camino Francés — three weeks on the classic pilgrim road.
