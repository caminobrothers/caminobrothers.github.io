What is this page?

This is the review page for our book, Buen Camino, Brother: A WhatsApp Story. Chapter 1 is our WhatsApp chat from Camino 1 (the Portuguese, October 2019). When the book starts for real, this is where we read through each chapter together and decide what stays and what goes — one chapter at a time.

How reviewing works

  1. Enter the password below, then tap your name. Mike is blue, Nicki is gold — so you can always see whose mark is whose. You can switch names any time to try the other side.
  2. Read through the messages. On each one, tap Keep (fine to print), Cut (suggest leaving it out) or Veto (never in print — no reason needed, and a veto from either of us wins). Nothing is ever deleted; a cut can always be undone.
  3. Messages marked ⚑ Sensitive touch on family, health, money or other people. They stay out unless we both tap Keep.
  4. You can also suggest different wording under any message, and tap Discuss to talk about a specific one in the chat box.
  5. When you've been through the whole chapter, tap “I'm done” at the top. A chapter counts as finished only when we both have.

Demo note: this copy is just to show how it will work. Marks you make here stay on this device and don't reach the other person yet — the real page will sync us live.
